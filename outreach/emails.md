# Silsila: Resonance - outreach emails

Drafts for getting the event into listings, newsletters and coverage.
Chhandayan and Abrons are handled separately.

---

## The format

Every email follows the same five-part shape. The order matters: the ask comes
before the story, because a listings editor decides in two lines whether to keep
reading.

1. **Subject** - event name, date, city. No teaser subjects.
2. **Two sentences** - who you are, what the event is.
3. **The ask** - one specific request. Never "let us know if you're interested."
4. **Details block** - plain text, copy-pasteable. Never only in an image.
5. **Assets line** - say what is attached and grant permission to use it.

Attach: `poster-web.jpg`, plus `assets/email/` banners if you want them to have
the ready-made graphics. Paste the invitation below your signature for warm
contacts only.

## Blurbs for listing forms

**40 words**

> Sitarist Jayanta Banerjee and tabla maestro Pandit Samir Chatterjee headline an
> evening of Hindustani classical music, joined by a sitar duet with disciple
> Tehreem Khan and a seven-artist finale bridging Eastern and Western traditions.

**100 words**

> Silsila means continuation; resonance is the note that lingers after it is
> played. Presented by Ruh Arts Foundation, this evening traces the living journey
> of South Asian classical music. Internationally acclaimed sitarist Jayanta
> Banerjee and Pandit Samir Chatterjee, a virtuoso of the Farrukhabad tabla
> gharana, open in conversation. A sitar duet between Banerjee and his disciple
> Tehreem Khan honours the guru-shishya bond that has carried this music across
> generations. The night closes with A Dialogue Between East and West, a
> collaborative finale composed by Banerjee, uniting sitar, tabla, violin,
> bansuri, keyboard and dholak.

## Sending notes

- **Send individually.** No group sends, no BCC blasts. These are small
  organisations and a personal note gets answered.
- **Attach, don't inline.** Attach `poster-web.jpg` so it survives image
  blocking. Keep the details as text in the body.
- **Follow up once**, about five days later, two sentences.
- **Track replies** so nobody gets chased twice.
