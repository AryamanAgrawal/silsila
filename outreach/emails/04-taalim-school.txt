Dear Taalim School,

I'm writing from Ruh Arts Foundation, a 501(c)(3) working to safeguard the Subcontinent's classical arts. On Sunday 30 August we're presenting Silsila: Resonance at the Playhouse Theater, Abrons Arts Center.

Silsila means continuation, and the evening is built around exactly that. Its centrepiece is a sitar duet between Jayanta Banerjee and his disciple Tehreem Khan, honouring the guru-shishya bond that carries this music from one generation to the next. Jayanta opens the evening on sitar with Pandit Samir Chatterjee on tabla, and it closes with a finale he composed for seven musicians across sitar, tabla, violin, bansuri, keyboard and dholak.

Given that Taalim is built on that same teacher-to-student transmission, I thought your students and their families might find something in it. Would you be willing to share it with your community?

DELIBERATE FRAMING. Taalim is a tabla school with its own guru, Pandit Divyang Vakil, and its own system, Taalvidya. Samir Chatterjee runs Chhandayan, a peer tabla institution in the same metro area. Asking one tabla school to promote a concert headlined by another school's founder can land badly.

So this email leads with the guru-shishya duet rather than with Samir. That is honest - the sitar duet genuinely is the centrepiece - and it is also the angle most aligned with Taalim, whose whole pedagogy is built on teacher-to-student transmission. Samir appears in the lineup where he belongs, without being the pitch.

Do not describe Samir as a teacher or reference his faculty positions here.

GROUP RATE: left out, because it is still unconfirmed. If Tehreem confirms one is available, add a line after the ask: "We would also be glad to arrange a group rate if a number of your students would like to come together." That would meaningfully raise the chance of a yes.

CALIBRATION: this is the least certain email in the set. Schools do not always promote other institutions' artists. Worth sending because it costs nothing and the guru-shishya framing gives it a real chance, but do not chase it.
