Dear Indo-American Arts Council,

I'm writing from Ruh Arts Foundation, a 501(c)(3) dedicated to safeguarding the Subcontinent's classical arts. We're a young organisation working toward much the same thing IAAC has been doing for years, and I'm hoping we can help each other.
