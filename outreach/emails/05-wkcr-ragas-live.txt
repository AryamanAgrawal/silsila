Hi David,

Jayanta Banerjee released Echoes to Sky this year, and on 30 August he performs from it live for the first time, at the Playhouse Theater, Abrons Arts Center, alongside Pandit Samir Chatterjee on tabla.

The programme moves from a traditional sitar-and-tabla set into a composition of Jayanta's own for sitar, tabla, violin, bansuri, keyboard and dholak, with a guru-shishya sitar duet in between.

Would Ragas Live or WKCR be interested in an on-air mention, or in having Jayanta and Samir in for a short interview before the date? Both are in New York and available.
