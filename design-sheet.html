Three things to resolve

The date does not agree with itself. The poster reads Saturday, August 30th, 2026. In 2026, August 30 is a Sunday; the Saturday is August 29. Which is right? Everything downstream (schema.org markup, the calendar file, the marquee) keys off this.
“Players Theatre” is probably the Playhouse. The poster says Players Theatre - 466 Grand St. 466 Grand St is Abrons Arts Center, whose rooms are the Playhouse (~314 seats), the Experimental Theater, and the Underground Theater. There is a Players Theatre in NYC, but it is at 115 MacDougal. I will write “Abrons Arts Center · Playhouse” unless you say otherwise.
Pranav Shikarpur is on the poster but not in the blurb. Page 5 credits him on bansuri. Your programme description lists violin, keyboard and dholak for the finale. I am building the lineup as seven artists with Pranav included. Confirm he is in.
What I did not find, and will need. Ticket URL or a “tickets on sale soon” state, ticket price tiers, a run time, and one wide photograph of the full ensemble if one exists (nice for the hero, not required). Also: is the Echoes to Sky album embed welcome on this page? I have the DistroKid link.

Material

The poster is a physical object: taped photographs on painted plaster, torn kraft, a paperclip, a checkerboard border. That physicality is the identity, and it is also the thing most likely to be lost in translation to the web. These are the five materials, all real extracted assets.

Plaster wash

texture/plaster-wash.jpg - centred on mid-grey, overlay-blended onto --plaster. Fixed-attachment on large fields so it reads as a wall, not a tile.

Paper grain

texture/paper-grain.jpg - overlay onto --bone. Subtle enough to survive a phone screen, present enough to kill the flat-white feel.

Kraft tape & tear

Three tape variants and a torn strip, alpha recovered from the PDF soft masks. Rotated per instance so no two are identical.

Checkerboard

Vector on the poster, so rebuilt in CSS rather than extracted. Two scales: 26px on bone, 22px inverted on plaster. It is the site's section divider and the loading motif.

Polaroid

Rebuilt as a CSS component rather than the extracted frame image, so it scales and reflows. White stock, 14px rails, 52px chin, two-layer drop shadow, greyscale + 6% contrast on the photograph.

05

Motion

GSAP, with one governing idea rather than a bag of effects: the site behaves like a struck string. Nothing on this page eases linearly to a stop. Things are plucked, they overshoot, and they settle with a decay you can feel. That is what the word resonance is asking for, and it is a motion language nobody else's concert site is using. Every demo below is live - press replay.

Also proposed, not demoed above

Move Where Why
Magnetic + resonance ring Ticket button (try the one in section 04) Cursor attraction inside 60px, and a ring that expands and decays on click. Same decay curve as the strings.
Plaster wipe Bone-to-plaster section joins The red field wipes up over the bone one rather than cutting. Masked, 0.7s, no layout shift.
Photograph settle All polaroids on scroll ScrollTrigger with once: true. They never re-animate on scroll back, which is what makes a page feel calm rather than busy.
Marquee scrub Detail strips Base drift plus a scroll-velocity term, so fast scrolling pushes the strip. Cheap, and it makes the page feel physical.
Counter to date Hero, on load The date numerals roll from 00 to 30 with the same decay. One second, once, never again.
Restraint, stated as a rule. No smooth-scroll hijacking (Lenis included). No WebGL. No page-transition library. Everything above respects prefers-reduced-motion by collapsing to a 200ms fade, and the page is fully readable with JavaScript off. The event is 90 minutes of two people listening to each other very carefully - the site should not be louder than that.

07

Stack

Layer Choice
Framework Astro 6, zero client framework. Same setup as aryamanagrawal.github.io.
Styling Plain CSS with the tokens above in src/styles/tokens.css. No Tailwind - this is one page with a strong opinion, utilities would fight it.
Motion GSAP 3.13 + ScrollTrigger + SplitText + DrawSVG, all free as of 3.13. One island, deferred.
Fonts Self-hosted woff2 via Fontsource. Anton, Cormorant Garamond, Pinyon Script, Ephesis, Open Sauce One. Subset to latin, preload the two used above the fold.
Images astro:assets, AVIF + WebP, responsive srcset. Photographs are already greyscale so they compress hard.
SEO schema.org MusicEvent with performers, offers and location, so the date shows up in Google and Apple Maps. OG image generated from the poster.
Hosting Static. Netlify or GitHub Pages on a custom domain, whichever you prefer.

Performance targets

Lighthouse 100 / 100 / 100 / 100 on mobile. Under 200KB on first paint excluding the hero photograph. LCP under 1.2s on 4G. The whole GSAP bundle is 48KB gzipped and loads after first paint, so the page is readable before any of the motion arrives.

Accessibility

All motion collapses under prefers-reduced-motion. Every polaroid has a real alt. The bio drawers are <details> underneath, so they work without JavaScript. Contrast pairs are the measured ones in section 01, and the two failing pairs are impossible to hit because plaster and sindoor never touch as foreground and background.
